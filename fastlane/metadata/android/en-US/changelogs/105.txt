This release adds support for the per-app language setting in Android 13 and later. In settings System / Languages / App Languages you can pick the language that Sonant should use, from those available.

The languages available in Sonant are those which have been translated to 40% or more.
You can help translating Sonant at https://hosted.weblate.org/engage/mumla/

Sonant was recently adapted for running on Android 14 (SDK 34). Hopefully it is not less stable than before.
