This is a translation release. Sonant is now available in Tamil, thank you TamilNeram.

On Android 13 and later, Sonant nowdays supports per-app language setting. Pick language in System settings / Languages / App Languages.

The languages available in Sonant are those which have been translated to 40% or more.
You can help translating Sonant at https://hosted.weblate.org/engage/mumla/
